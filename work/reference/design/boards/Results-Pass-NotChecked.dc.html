Results stay quiet until you ask for them

Problems are what a reviewer acts on, so they keep the sheet to themselves. Passed and not-checked cells add one line of counts to the errors panel by default. Marks on the sheet are one switch away, and the host can turn results off entirely. When marks are on, they are a 9 px glyph in a cell's bottom-left corner: no fill, no pin, no badge count.

What keeps it from being overwhelming

No fill, no border, no pinA result never tints a cell. Only a problem does, so a sea of passes stays white.
Never in a count that asks for actionTab counts, the Download badge and the panel's header count are problems only.
A problem always winsA cell with both a pass and a problem shows the problem.
Off the sheet by defaultSummary is the default; marks are one switch, shared by the pane and remembered per pane while it lives.
Grouped, not listed180 passes are two rows in the list, one per check, opened on demand.
Not in the download by defaultComments export writes results only with includeResults (W-50 DoD 5).
