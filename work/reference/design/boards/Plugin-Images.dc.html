Images — pictures sit where Excel put them and scroll with their cells

A photo anchored across C4:F9 lives in the overlay, in sheet space. Formats a browser can't draw (EMF, WMF) get a labelled frame of the same size, so nothing on the sheet silently disappears.

View and Edit differ only in what sits above: in Edit, pins and the selection bar draw over pictures, and a click on a picture selects the cell underneath so it can still be flagged.
